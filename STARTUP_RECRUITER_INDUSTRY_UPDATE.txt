United Frontier v16 — Startup, Recruiters and Industrial Supply

Use this ZIP in place of the previous v16 Player UI Update. All of that build's UI and gameplay fixes are included.
Startup: fixed a mismatched resource list name in the resource marker helper that caused a startup failure when resource markers were initialized.
Recruiters: the host can type a positive whole number above 25 for Level 1 production. Levels multiply the base as before. Readiness, unrest, ideology and exercise modifiers still apply.
Optional industry: enable "Power Grids and Chip Manufacturing" in the host's military settings. Resources and Military Systems must also be on. Power Grid costs 150 Commerce + 1 Copper. Chip Manufacturing costs 250 Commerce + 1 Iron and requires a Power Grid anywhere in the nation. Both buildings have one fixed icon and no upgrade levels. A powered plant converts 1 Rare Earths into 1 chip per turn, up to 10 stored per nation. A chip is used automatically for a 25% Commerce discount on an Air Wing. Air Wings remain available without chips at the regular price.
Verification: full startup checks on a local 40-player fixture, Lua syntax, host config save above 25, recruiter scaling, industrial purchases and stockpile accounting, discounted and normal Air Wing purchases, and map marker checks. Live War.app test still needed.
