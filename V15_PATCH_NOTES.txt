UNITED FRONTIER V15 — CONVENTIONAL MILITARY

Removed missile sites, all missile purchases and launches, salvos, nuclear and
EMP strikes, interceptors, agencies, intelligence operations, financial espionage,
cyber operations, counterintelligence, tracing, operation notifications and reports.
Removed their AI actions, configuration controls, menu entries and help text.
Retired Power Grids; Airbases and Air Wings operate without a grid prerequisite.

Military screen: Airbases (airports), Forward Airstrips, Air Wings, Special Forces,
Tanks and Drones. Recruiters remain available because they produce regular armies.
Existing markets, resources, recruitment, diplomacy, factions and UN systems remain.

NEW UNITS (host can change Commerce, strength and national limits)
Tank: 300 Commerce + 1 Oil + 1 Iron; strength 8; limit 10 per nation.
Drone: 150 Commerce + 1 Oil; strength 2; limit 10 per nation.
Tank deployment: any owned territory.
Drone deployment: owned Airbase or Forward Airstrip.
Strength sets attack, defense, damage-to-kill and damage absorption.
Purchases deploy when the turn advances, then use normal movement and combat.
They follow normal fog and cannot be gifted/transferred to teammates.
No separate Drone recon or remote attack missions are included.
Limits count real units on the current map plus pending purchases.
AI can buy them within its normal wartime spending budget and resource stockpile.

Mobile military screen uses full-width actions, readable costs and confirmation.
How It Works describes the current systems and exact numerical examples.

EXISTING GAMES
On the next server action or turn, migration discards obsolete inventories,
queued strikes/covert operations, agency reports, alerts and disruptions.
Obsolete map structures are removed when the turn finishes. Compatibility PNGs
remain in the archive only so old map structures can load until cleanup runs.
Old research/discovery caches are rebuilt from ownership, borders and sharing.
This retirement does not refund historical construction/research purchases.
Economy, holdings, companies, airport levels and existing combat units are kept.

Install the complete set of eight Lua files, including NEW
UF_ConventionalMilitary.lua, plus the two image directories.
