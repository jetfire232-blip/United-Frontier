UNITED FRONTIER V4 — STRATEGY / USABILITY PASS

Major additions in this build:
- Infrastructure HP scales by level: 10 / 20 / 30 / 40 max HP.
- Upgrades preserve existing damage and refresh one structure icon only; no duplicate airport/silo icon is intended.
- Airbase resource cost: host-configured Oil + Iron per level (defaults 1 + 1).
- Forward Airstrip resource cost: host-configured Oil per level (default 1).
- Human + AI Airlift Cards require enabled airport infrastructure at both endpoints when Airbase/Airstrip systems are enabled. If both are disabled, normal War.app airlift rules apply.
- Intelligence Agency replaces Cyber Facility wording. One physical Agency per nation; rebuild is allowed after loss/capture.
- Intelligence Operations Lv.1–3, Cyber Lv.1–2, Counterintelligence Lv.1–2.
- Financial Espionage at Intelligence Lv.3.
- Detected covert operations create same-turn COUNTER / TRACE / IGNORE choices. AI defenders can spend Commerce on the same active countermeasure.
- Faction intelligence report sharing.
- Missile Command & Defense quantity buying/launching, Interceptor stockpile, host-configurable interception chances, Lv.3 intelligence missile warnings.
- Faction directory, join requests, chat, leader faction-war declarations, alerts, ideology-aware AI faction creation/join logic.
- Host-configurable Faction Exercise contribution tiers and bonuses with hard troop-output previews.
- AI Manager recurring + one-time extra budget and simplified screen.
- Central Military Reports screen.
- How It Works rewritten for the current systems.

Build basis: United-Frontier-Full-Overhaul-v3-Faction-PrivateGridCyber-UraniumSilos.zip
