UNITED FRONTIER V16 — RESOURCE DIRECTORY / AIRSTRIP REMOVAL

Upload this build to UNITED FRONTIER only.

1. Keep a backup of your existing source.
2. Extract the ZIP.
3. Upload all eight Lua files into your mod root, including
   UF_ConventionalMilitary.lua.
4. Upload both StructureImages and SpecialUnitImages with those exact names.
5. Reload/update your mod code using your normal workflow.
6. Open Resources: choose MY RESOURCES, NEIGHBORS, ALLY / FACTION or ALL NATIONS,
   then choose a resource type. SHOW ON MAP highlights the selected territory.
