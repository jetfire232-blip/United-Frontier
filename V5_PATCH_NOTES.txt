V7 ACQUISITIONS + MOBILE TRADE + CALLBACK FIX

- Fixed the player-overview crash: CLOSE now passes a normal function wrapper instead of a CallbackFunction value.
- Acquisition screens now show the target market cap, rank, active-company average, exact offer formula, estimated shareholder payout per share, and the buyer flagship's projected stock-price and confidence changes.
- Completed acquisitions automatically cash out every shareholder. Each shareholder receives a notice showing shares, Commerce received, and realized value per share.
- The seller receives the unpaid remainder plus any payout on shares they personally held, with a complete sale-price breakdown and notification.
- The buyer receives a purchase confirmation with the exact stock-price and confidence change. The market news entry includes the purchase price and settlement totals.
- Acquisition rounding is floored and capped so shareholder payments can never create more Commerce than the buyer paid.
- Trade Agreements filters now use two equal-width buttons per row. Each partner is a vertical mobile card with a full-width PROPOSE TRADE button.
- AI Manager status and spending are split into short lines; budget presets use two equal-width buttons per row.
