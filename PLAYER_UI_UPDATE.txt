United Frontier v16 — Player UI Update

Based on: United-Frontier-v16-Turn1-Diplomacy-Runtime-Fix.
Changed Lua files: Client_PresentMenuUI.lua, Server_AdvanceTurn.lua, Server_StartGame.lua.

National setup uses five guided choices followed by review. Economic reform uses the same flow and preserves the flagship name. Illustrative stock and recruiter numbers are labeled as examples.
Markets groups stock purchases and ETF, portfolio, rankings/news, and trade/investment tools. Once rankings refresh, the public board shows troop and Commerce reserve ranges; it does not publish exact ranking totals.
Diplomacy player profiles have Summary, Portfolio, and Relations tabs. Portfolio shows holdings and market value; the other player's cost basis and performance are not shown on this screen.
With Airports enabled, the Airlift Card requires a working airport at both ends, including in large matches. With Airports off, normal Airlift Card rules apply.
Eligible UN council AIs vote automatically. A missing human council vote is filled at the deadline using the automatic vote rules, but an automatic human NO cannot exercise a veto. A submitted abstention counts as a choice and will not be changed.
Resource facility levels affect production and remain visible in the resource directory. Map icons stay at one marker per resource territory with a fixed base icon. Legacy markers are reconciled during turn processing.
Power Grid and chip manufacturing are not included in this build.

Verification: all Lua syntax checks passed, with local callback simulations for setup, rankings and portfolios, Airlift, UN voting, unrest tiers, company confidence, and resource markers. Still needs a live War.app mobile/turn test before release.
