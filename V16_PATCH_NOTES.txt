UNITED FRONTIER V16

Resources are listed publicly in the menu. No Agency or intelligence discovery
is required. Nation-group and resource-type filters, pagination, ownership labels,
SHOW ON MAP and owned-facility upgrades are available.

Uranium is removed from active resource generation, building, trade selection,
stockpiles, maintenance and host settings. Legacy data is cleaned on the next
server action/turn; paid pending Uranium builds are refunded exactly once.

Remaining resources:
Oil: Airport/Tank/Drone construction and military maintenance.
Iron: Airport/Tank construction and military maintenance.
Food: military maintenance and unrest.
Gas: national readiness and Commerce maintenance.
Coal/Copper: Commerce maintenance.
Rare Earths/Lithium: readiness and Commerce maintenance.
The menu explains current production, need, stockpile, net and shortage effects.
Each Recruiter level adds 1 Oil, 1 Iron and 1 Food demand per turn.
Tank purchase: 300 Commerce + 1 Oil + 1 Iron; default combat strength 8.
Drone purchase: 150 Commerce + 1 Oil; deploy at Airport; default strength 2.

Forward Airstrips are removed from building, AI, menus, settings and Airlift rules.
Airports are the only eligible airfield for Airlift Cards when Airports are enabled.
Power Grids remain removed. Airports do not require power infrastructure.
Airport artwork and existing upgrade rules are unchanged.
Recruiter icon replacement uses structure-type equality, preventing stacked tiers.
A merged end-of-turn map update preserves resource, Airport and Recruiter icons
on the same territory and clears obsolete structures.

Missile, Agency, cyber, counterintelligence and sharing-request UI remain removed.
How It Works, host controls and resource explanations match the current systems.
