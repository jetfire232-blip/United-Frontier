United Frontier — Olympics Showcase

Host: On Turn 1, open Olympics and choose the sports and schedule. With
betting enabled, set the first and last betting turns or leave them blank
to use every turn between training and results. Both dates are inclusive.
Training through Turn 4, betting Turns 6–8, and results on Turn 10
leaves Turns 5 and 9 closed to both training and bets.
Already published schedules keep the dates they were published with.

Players: Each sport has its own venue image and finish headline. After
results, select a sport and tap Replay Medal Reveal to see its official
medals in reverse order. Replaying never rerolls or changes rewards.
Tap Refresh Betting Odds to fetch the current pool during betting. The
chance of gold is based on eligible teams and training. The payout
multiplier depends on how the pool is split and can change before close.
Displayed returns include the winning stake. Only in-game Commerce is used.

Install: Keep UIImages at the mod root alongside the Lua files and the
existing image directories. The six venue images are included.
