United Frontier v16 - AI Manager one-time payment

PAY & RESERVE FOR NEXT TURN deducts gold immediately and records prepaid funds.
Only one payment can be pending; repeated clicks do not charge again.
At the next turn the payment is released into normal spending accounting once.
Manager purchases deduct normally, so the player is not charged twice.
Unspent money returns to available gold after processing. Paused managers or
host-disabled managers return the funding during next-turn processing.
Recurring budgets remain spending limits, not advance payments.
Existing unpaid one-time authorizations remain spending limits until processed;
only payments confirmed using this update create prepaid reserved funds.

Example excluding other income/spending: start 1,000, pay 500 = 500 available
and 500 reserved. Manager spends 300 total next turn = 700 available afterward.

Required replacements: Client_PresentMenuUI.lua, Server_GameCustomMessage.lua,
Server_AdvanceTurn.lua, UF_ConventionalMilitary.lua.
