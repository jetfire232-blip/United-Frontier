UNITED FRONTIER — AIR WING COMBAT STRENGTH

Air Wing combat strength now defaults to 25.

One Air Wing now uses 25 attack, 25 defense, 25 health (damage to kill), and 25 damage absorption. It therefore fights and survives roughly like 25 normal armies.

The host can change this from 1 to 50 using the new Air Wing Combat Strength setting.

Aircraft Represented per Air Wing remains a separate display-only setting and defaults to 25 aircraft.
