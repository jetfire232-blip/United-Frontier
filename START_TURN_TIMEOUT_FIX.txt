United Frontier v16 - Start Turn Timeout Fix

The screenshot identified ModContainerTimeout in Server_AdvanceTurn_Start.
This update reduces the major repeated map work in that hook:
- PrivateGameData and PlayerGameData are now loaded once before the nation
  loops and saved once after processing. Strategic AI no longer performs
  serialized data reads and writes separately for every AI nation.
- The conventional-system migration shares that same in-memory snapshot instead
  of loading and saving all three mod data stores again at every turn start.
- All Army Recruiters now add their reinforcements through one batched event.
  A 40-player match can therefore replace up to 120 separate deploy submissions
  with one server submission while producing the same armies on the same land.
- In matches with 25 or more players, the mod's extra AI military orders are
  divided evenly between turns. War.app's normal AI still acts every turn.
- AI wartime routing builds one route map per nation instead of running a new
  breadth-first map search from every strong interior territory.
- Existing turn cache supplies owned territories and war status to military,
  Gift Card, Airlift, recruiter, airport, Tank and Drone planning.
- Resource map icons are no longer refreshed in both Start and End; the unified
  End refresh continues to synchronize resources, Recruiters and Airports.

AI behavior remains: choose its strongest worthwhile attack, then move one
strong interior stack one step toward the nearest frontline. Economy, resource,
diplomacy and unit rules are unchanged.

Local regression confirms Recruiter production is batched, large-match military
work is staggered, the attack and transfer remain correct, and the route search
is near-linear. This is not a live War.app timing measurement.
Test the current game through at least two advances before approval.

Includes the End timeout fix, AI Manager payment fix, startup capitalization
fix and all eight resource icons. Image folders are PNG-only. Replace all eight
Lua files for a clean synchronized installation.
