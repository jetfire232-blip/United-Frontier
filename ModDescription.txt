United Frontier
A modern economy, diplomacy, resources, intelligence, and strategic-warfare expansion for War.app.

Build your nation through Commerce, markets, strategic resources, alliances, intelligence, military infrastructure, and war.

Current development includes cleaner Overview/Global Economy UI, resource offers and requests, color-coded resources, public/border resource visibility rules, capped resource facility icons, configurable UN sanctions/ceasefire durations, and the Phase 2 military foundation.


Phase 4: player-specific strategic resource intelligence, hidden military infrastructure storage, Headquarters construction/upgrades, Airbases, Forward Airstrips, SAM Sites, Missile Silos, public Power Grids, Air Wings, and Special Forces purchasing foundations.
